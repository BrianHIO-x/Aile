Aile 的基础词库（base_lexicon.txt）与二元模型（bigram.txt）除 AOSP PinyinIME 原始词库外（见
NOTICE-AOSP-PinyinIME.txt），还使用了以下数据生成。词表、读音与词频经过合并、重新统计与筛选，
各数据的原始文件不随应用分发，生成方法见 tools/build-lexicon.mjs。

The base lexicon (base_lexicon.txt) and the bigram model (bigram.txt) of Aile are derived, in addition to the
AOSP PinyinIME raw dictionary (see NOTICE-AOSP-PinyinIME.txt), from the following data. Word lists, readings and
frequencies were merged, re-counted and filtered; the original files are not distributed with the app.

- jieba (dict.txt), https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba

- phrase-pinyin-data, https://github.com/mozillazg/phrase-pinyin-data
  Copyright (c) 2017 mozillazg

  Copyright (c) 2016 mozillazg
- THUOCL: Tsinghua Open Chinese Lexicon, https://github.com/thunlp/THUOCL
  Copyright (c) 2018 THUNLP
